Help & Support

Quick guide to the Social platform
SUPPORT
S

Welcome to Smart Money Social

Discover creators, share photos and text, and connect with the community.

📸 Posts & photos

The main feed is for text and photo posts. Video uploads are not part of the feed.

⭕ Stories

Stories are the planned place for short photo or video updates and expire after 24 hours.

💬 Community

Member discovery and private text conversations will be connected after accounts are enabled.

🔐 Login & privacy

Login, signup and guest access are intentionally unavailable during this build phase.

🚩 Safety

Respect others, share content you have permission to post, and never publish private information.

🛠️ Preview status

Some buttons are visual previews. Posting, follows and messaging will be connected after the structure is complete.

Community guidelines

  • Be respectful and do not harass other members.
  • Share original content or media you have permission to use.
  • Do not post passwords, private information or personal contact details.
  • Do not spam or impersonate others.
  • Use reporting and moderation responsibly when available.
