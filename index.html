Help & Support

Smart Money Social help desk and contact details
SUPPORT
S

Welcome to Smart Money Social

Need help with your account, posts, stories or messages? Follow the guide below or email our support team.

📸 Posts & photos

Open Home and tap Create Post or the Photo option. Write your message, choose a photo if needed, review it and submit.

⭕ Stories

Tap Add Story, select your photo or video, add any text you want and publish. Stories are designed to expire after 24 hours.

💬 Messages

Open Messages from the bottom navigation, choose the person you want to contact, type your message and tap Send. Keep conversations respectful.

🔎 Search & profiles

Use Search to find people, open a profile to view available details, and use Follow or Unfollow where those controls are available.

🔐 Account & privacy

Open Menu → Settings & Privacy to review available account, interaction and content preferences. Never share your password or verification codes.

🚩 Report a problem

If something is not working, email support. Explain which page or button has the issue and what happened. You may attach a screenshot, but hide private information first.

📩 Contact Help & Support

Email us with a clear subject, for example “Login problem”, “Post upload issue” or “Account help”. In your message, include your display name, a short explanation of the issue, the page/button involved and the steps you already tried. Please do not send passwords, OTPs or other sign-in secrets.

About Smart Money

Our vision, learning philosophy and digital ecosystem
ABOUT SMART MONEY

Welcome to Smart Money

Smart Money is a learning-focused digital initiative created around a simple idea: useful knowledge, practical skills and accessible technology can help people become more confident in the digital world. It aims to bring learning, creativity, digital tools and community experiences under one identity. Rather than presenting technology as something complicated or distant, Smart Money seeks to explain useful ideas in a clear, approachable way and encourage people to keep developing their abilities.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a connected digital environment where people can discover learning resources, practise creative skills, explore technology and exchange ideas. The digital world changes quickly. New tools, platforms and ways of working appear all the time. Smart Money believes that continuous learning, curiosity and responsible use of technology are valuable in responding to those changes. We want the platform and its learning resources to be understandable to beginners while remaining useful to people who already have experience.

Learning and skill development

Learning is an important part of the Smart Money identity. Educational material may cover practical digital subjects such as computer and internet basics, communication, writing, content planning, data handling, creative production, editing workflows and the responsible use of modern technology. The exact topics, lessons and availability can change as resources are developed. Our aim is to make information easier to understand by breaking broad subjects into smaller concepts, examples and practical steps. Learning takes time, and progress depends on each person's interests, effort, access to tools and circumstances.

Creativity and digital work

Smart Money also values creativity. Digital skills are not limited to technical tasks: they can support storytelling, design, music, video, photography, writing and many other forms of expression. We encourage people to develop their own ideas, respect the work of others and share only material they own or have permission to use. Tools can support a creative process, but thoughtful decisions, originality and practice remain important.

Technology with responsibility

Technology should be useful, understandable and handled responsibly. Smart Money may introduce digital tools, explain common workflows and share information about online safety, privacy and good digital habits. Technology can make certain tasks easier, but it can also create risks. Users should check information before relying on it, protect account credentials, understand the permissions they grant and be cautious about unfamiliar links, downloads or requests for personal information.

Community and collaboration

A strong learning environment benefits from respectful conversation. Smart Money aims to encourage a community culture where people can ask questions, share experiences, offer constructive feedback and learn from different perspectives. Participation should be respectful. Harassment, impersonation, spam, deceptive claims and sharing another person's private information have no place in a healthy community. Community features may be offered through Smart Money Social and other services as they become available.

About services, opportunities and expectations

Smart Money may develop or provide different educational resources, digital services, creative tools or community features over time. Each service should be understood according to its own description, terms, eligibility requirements, pricing and availability. A mention of a skill, course, tool or opportunity is not a promise of employment, clients, sales, profit or a particular result. Outcomes vary with individual circumstances and external factors. Smart Money does not guarantee income or financial returns, and general educational content should not be treated as personal financial, legal, tax or investment advice. Before paying for a service or joining an opportunity, read the relevant details carefully and ask questions if anything is unclear.

Our values

We value clarity, continuous learning, creativity, respect, privacy, responsible technology use and honest communication. We aim to explain what a service does, avoid misleading promises and improve our resources as we learn from feedback. Not every idea or feature will be available immediately; the ecosystem may evolve, and descriptions should be read together with the current service information.

Moving forward

Smart Money is a developing digital initiative. Its identity is broader than any single page or application: it represents an ongoing effort to make learning and digital participation more accessible. We invite users to explore available resources thoughtfully, practise at their own pace and contribute positively wherever community features are provided.

Smart Money — Learn, create, grow and connect responsibly.

About Smart Money Social

A place to share, discover and connect
ABOUT SOCIAL

Welcome to Smart Money Social

Smart Money Social is the community and social-sharing experience within the wider Smart Money identity. It is designed to give members a place to express ideas, share permitted content, discover other creators and take part in community conversations. Smart Money is the broader learning and digital initiative; Smart Money Social has a more focused role: bringing people and their shared content together in a single community space.

What you can do

Depending on the features currently enabled, members may be able to create text updates and photo posts, publish short-lived stories, browse a feed, explore creators, follow accounts, react to posts, write comments, save content and use messaging or notification features. The available tools can change as the service is improved. Some functions may be in preview, limited, or temporarily unavailable. The controls visible in the app and the latest notices should be treated as the current guide to what is operational.

Your profile and identity

Your profile helps other members recognise your account. It may contain a display name, username, profile image, bio and other details you choose to add. Keep your profile information appropriate and avoid including sensitive details such as passwords, financial account information, private addresses or personal documents. Use an account name and image that you are entitled to use, and do not impersonate another person, creator or organisation.

Posts, photos and stories

Content is central to a social community. Before publishing a post, photo or story, make sure you have the rights and permission needed to share it. Think about who may be able to see it and whether it reveals information about you or someone else. Stories and other temporary formats may be designed to expire or become unavailable after a set period, but temporary visibility should not be treated as a guarantee that nobody can capture or retain a copy. Do not share content that violates another person's privacy, intellectual property or safety.

Discovering and interacting with people

Discovery and interaction features can help members find creators and take part in conversations. Follow, like, comment and message tools should be used thoughtfully. A disagreement is not a reason to harass someone. Do not send repeated unwanted messages, threaten other members, spread rumours as facts or use the platform to deceive people. Respect another member's boundaries and the privacy choices available in the app.

Safety, reporting and moderation

A useful community depends on members treating one another fairly. Do not post threats, targeted harassment, hate, scams, spam, impersonation, private information or content that you do not have permission to publish. If reporting or blocking tools are available, use them for genuine safety and policy concerns. Platform operators may review reported material and take appropriate action under the applicable community rules. Feature availability and response times may vary, and no online service can prevent every harmful interaction.

Privacy and account security

Review your privacy and audience settings before sharing. Use a strong, unique password where password login is provided, never share one-time codes or sign-in links, and sign out of devices you do not control. Smart Money Social may rely on hosting, database and media-storage providers to operate its features. The Privacy Policy explains the categories of information that may be processed and the choices available to members.

How Social relates to Smart Money

Smart Money Social is one part of the wider Smart Money ecosystem. Smart Money focuses on learning, digital skills, creativity and technology-related resources, while Social focuses on member profiles, sharing and community interaction. The two identities are connected, but they are not interchangeable. A social profile or community interaction does not automatically enrol a member in a course, paid service or other Smart Money programme. Any separate service has its own information, requirements and terms.

Respectful participation

We encourage original contributions, helpful discussion and a welcoming atmosphere for beginners and experienced creators alike. Give credit when it is due, ask permission before sharing someone else's work and correct mistakes when you discover them. Do not assume that every profile is verified or that every post is accurate. Use your judgement and verify important information independently.

A developing community

Smart Money Social is being developed over time. Its design, features and policies may be updated to improve reliability, usability and safety. We aim to make changes carefully and keep the experience understandable. Thank you for helping create a community where people can share ideas, learn from one another and connect responsibly.

Smart Money Social — Share thoughtfully. Discover openly. Connect respectfully.

PRIVACY

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you visit or use Smart Money Social. Smart Money Social is part of the wider Smart Money identity, but this policy focuses on information processed through this social community website and its enabled features. Separate Smart Money services may have additional notices or terms. Please read this policy carefully. It is a general description of intended practices and should be updated if the platform's actual data flows, providers or features change.

1. Information you choose to provide

When you create or use an account, you may provide information such as a display name, username, email address or other sign-in identifier, profile photograph, biography and profile preferences. If you publish content, the information may include text, photos, story media, comments and other material you submit. If you contact support, the information you send in that request may also be processed so the issue can be understood and answered. Please do not submit passwords, one-time verification codes, payment card details or other highly sensitive information in public posts or support messages.

2. Content and activity information

To operate social features, the platform may process records associated with posts, likes, comments, follows, saved items, story interactions, messages, notifications, blocks, reports and privacy settings, where those features are enabled. Such information helps the app display content, remember preferences, support interactions and apply account or safety controls. The exact information processed depends on which features you use and how they are configured.

3. Technical information

When you access a website, hosting and service providers may process technical information needed to deliver pages and maintain security. Depending on provider configuration, this may include device or browser type, approximate network information such as an IP address, timestamps, error logs and diagnostic events. This policy does not claim that every possible analytics or tracking technology is currently active. Any analytics, cookies or similar technologies should be described here if they are added or enabled.

4. Why information is used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts; display profiles and content; provide feed, story, messaging and interaction features; remember settings; deliver notifications; respond to support requests; investigate reports; prevent spam, abuse and unauthorised access; troubleshoot errors; maintain backups and service reliability; improve the user experience; and meet applicable legal obligations. We aim to use information for purposes connected with providing, securing and improving the service.

5. Who can see your content

Information you publish may be visible to other members or to a wider audience depending on the page, feature and privacy controls. A public post or profile should be treated as information that others may view, copy or share. Private or restricted features should be used according to the audience controls shown in the app, but no online platform can promise that a recipient will never capture or redistribute information they can access. Before uploading, consider whether the content includes your location, contact details, school or workplace information, documents, faces of other people or other details that should remain private.

6. Service providers and storage

The website may use third-party infrastructure to host the application, store account records and manage uploaded media. The current implementation uses Supabase services for backend functions and data storage, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers may process information on behalf of the service to deliver their infrastructure. Their own privacy notices and terms also apply to their services. Provider arrangements and technical architecture may change; this policy should be reviewed whenever such changes materially affect personal information.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers that help operate the platform, disclosed when necessary to protect users or the service, or provided where required by valid legal process or applicable law. Content you intentionally publish is shared with the audience allowed by the feature and settings you choose. We do not describe public content as confidential merely because it appears on a social platform.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect account information and platform data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. Security depends on both platform safeguards and user behaviour. Choose a strong unique password where applicable, protect your email and device, do not share verification codes, and report suspicious activity. No website, storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account history, handle reports, resolve disputes, preserve security records, maintain backups or comply with legal obligations. Deletion from the visible interface may not immediately remove every backup or record held by a provider. Where account deletion or content deletion tools are available, follow the instructions in the app. You may also contact the platform through its available support channel to ask about access, correction or deletion. Requests may need verification before action can be taken.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on the features available, you may be able to edit profile details, change privacy preferences, remove content, manage interactions, block accounts or stop using the service. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights to request access, correction, deletion or other handling of personal information. Requests should be made through the official support channel shown in the platform. We may need to retain certain records where the law permits or requires it, or where they are necessary for security and dispute resolution.

11. Children and young users

Smart Money Social should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements and local laws. Young users should avoid publishing personal contact details, real-time location, school details or private images. Parents or guardians who have a concern about information submitted by a child may use the platform's available support channel to raise the issue. The platform should not be used to solicit sensitive information from children.

12. External links and other services

The platform may contain links to websites or services operated by others. We do not control those services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their privacy notices before providing information or using their features. This policy applies to Smart Money Social, not every external website linked from it.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the platform's features, providers or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate platform notice where practical. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.

14. Contact and questions

For general help, account-related questions, technical issues or feedback about Smart Money Social, contact our support team using either of the email addresses below. For privacy questions, data access, correction or deletion requests, clearly write “Privacy Request” in your subject line so the request can be identified and reviewed. Please include only the information needed to understand your request. Never send your password, one-time verification code or other sign-in secrets by email.
